# Cliff Side texture provenance

The `cliff-side-*-2k.jpg` files in this directory are the **Cliff Side** scan by
Dario Barresi, James Ray Cock, and Jenelle van Heerden, downloaded from Poly
Haven:

https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side

Poly Haven publishes this asset under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
public-domain dedication:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The current steep-rock material uses the 2K JPG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps,
plus the 1K ARM (ambient-occlusion/roughness/metalness) and displacement maps.
Experimental copies may also be present in this development workspace. Poly
Haven documents the captured face at a physical width of 1.8 metres.
